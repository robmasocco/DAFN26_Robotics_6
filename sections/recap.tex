\begin{frame}{Recap}
  \justifying
	\textbg{ROS 2} software is organized in \textbg{packages}, built by \texttt{colcon} invoking either \textbg{CMake} or \textbg{setuptools}.\\
	\bigskip
	\textbg{Messages} are the most basic, \textbg{one-way} communication paradigm.\\
	In the \textbg{DDS RMW}, ROS 2 topics resolve to \textbg{DDS topics}.\\
  In the \textbg{Zenoh RMW}, ROS 2 topics resolve to \textbg{Zenoh keys}.\\
	\bigskip
	Messages formats are defined in \textbg{interface files} which usually constitute entire packages.\\
	\bigskip
	This lecture is \href{https://github.com/robmasocco/DAFN26_Robotics_6}{\color{blue}\underline{here}}.
\end{frame}
